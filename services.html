Mobile Auto Diagnostic Services in East County San Diego

We do not guess. We scan, test, read live data and tell you exactly what is failing and what your options are. Every service below is performed at your location.

  • Check Engine Light & OBD-II Diagnostics

    A glowing or flashing check engine light is your car’s way of saying a system has moved outside its expected range. We pull the codes, then interpret them with live data and freeze-frame information so we can tell whether the fault is a failing sensor, a vacuum leak, an emissions-related issue or a deeper engine problem. We see a lot of catalyst inefficiency, EVAP leaks and oxygen-sensor codes in the older trucks and SUVs common around East County, and we know which ones are a real repair and which ones are a loose gas cap or worn filler neck.

  • Diesel Diagnostics — Cummins, Duramax & Powerstroke

    Diesel owners in Ramona, Poway and Alpine depend on their trucks for work and towing. We diagnose the systems that matter: DPF regeneration failures, DEF dosing problems, turbocharger underboost, glow-plug circuit faults, injector balance rates and high-pressure fuel issues. We also handle electrical draw and trailer-lighting problems that show up on ranch and work trucks. If your diesel has gone into limp mode or keeps throwing P20EE, P2463 or P0299 codes, we can tell you whether the fix is a sensor, a cleaning or a trip to a diesel shop for component work.

  • Pre-Purchase Vehicle Inspection

    Buying a used truck, SUV or hybrid in East County without a proper inspection is a gamble. We meet you at the seller’s location and check the engine, transmission, battery, brakes, tires and body structure. We scan for stored and pending codes that a cleared dash might be hiding, look at module health and check hybrid battery voltage on older Priuses and Camrys if the seller claims it is ready for another 100,000 miles. You get a clear report and a recommendation before you hand over cash.

  • Electrical System Diagnostics

    Battery drain, alternator not charging, headlights flickering or trailer lights not syncing? We test parasitic draw, voltage drop, grounds and circuit integrity. For ranch and horse-trailer owners, we also check trailer wiring, brake controllers and 7-pin connectors. East County’s mix of daily drivers, off-road builds and work rigs means we see everything from failed door-lock actuators to custom light bars wired through a switch that is pulling too much current. We find the real cause and explain it in plain English.

  • Emissions & Smog Failure Diagnostics

    Failing a smog test in California is frustrating, especially when the shop just hands you a printout. We run the same monitors and readiness checks, then determine whether the failure is a weak catalytic converter, leaking EVAP system, faulty oxygen sensor, air-fuel ratio problem or an incomplete drive cycle. If a monitor will not set, we advise on the specific driving pattern needed for your vehicle rather than charging for parts you may not need.

  • No-Start / No-Crank Diagnostics

    Nothing happens when you turn the key? Or it cranks but will not catch? We test battery voltage, starter draw, fuel pressure, ignition and injection to isolate the failure. In the hotter, dustier parts of East County, we see a lot of starter and battery failures, fuel pump relay issues, and crankshaft-position sensor failures. We bring the right meters and scope so you are not paying for parts thrown at a guess.

  • Hybrid Battery Health Assessment

    Hybrid taxis, commuters and fleet cars are common in East County. We check battery-pack voltage, block balance and temperature spread to determine if a high-voltage battery is failing or if a single module is out of spec. A battery that looks fine on the dash can have internal drift that will leave you stranded. We give you the numbers so you can decide whether to repair, recondition or replace.
