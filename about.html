About East County Mobile Auto Diagnostics

Real diagnostics from a technician who has been turning wrenches in San Diego County for more than four decades.

Four Decades of Diagnostic Experience

Modern cars are computers on wheels, but a scan tool only tells you what the computer is reporting. Knowing why it is reporting that value takes experience. We have 40+ years of diagnosing everything from 1970s carburetors to modern CAN-bus hybrids and diesel trucks with multiple ECUs.

East County is not like central San Diego. The vehicle mix is different here: lifted trucks used on weekends, diesel work rigs that tow horse trailers, older commuters that keep running because their owners maintain them, and hybrids that are finally aging into battery issues. The terrain is different too. Heat, dust and mountain grades stress engines, transmissions and cooling systems differently than stop-and-go city driving. That context changes what a code actually means.

We Come to Your Property

You do not need a tow. You do not need to schedule around a shop’s hours. We meet you at your home, your ranch, your job site or the parking lot where the truck broke down. If the vehicle cannot safely get to a shop, that is exactly when you call us.

Professional-Grade Tools

We use real diagnostic scan tools, multimeters, scopes and diesel-capable software. Not a $50 code reader from a big-box store. That difference matters when you are trying to separate a bad sensor from a bad wire, or a real injector problem from an electronic control issue.

Diesel and Gas Capable

From domestic light-duty diesels to gas-powered imports and Toyota hybrids, we have the software coverage and the experience to read the data correctly. We explain the findings and discuss practical repair options.

Diagnosis and Repair Options

Our job is to tell you what is wrong and what it will likely take to fix it. We can discuss an on-site repair when practical or recommend shop-based service when needed. There is no pressure and no hidden markup. You pay for the diagnostic, and you get a report you can hand to any shop or use yourself.

When to Call Us

Call when the check engine light is on, when a used-truck purchase feels risky, when the diesel is in limp mode, when the hybrid battery is acting up, or when something electrical keeps draining the battery. We bring the test equipment to you, read the data, and give you a straight answer. We provide mobile repairs and diagnostics; call to discuss the work your vehicle needs.

Mobile Repair Services

In addition to troubleshooting, contact us about brake pads and rotors, batteries, starters, alternators, cooling system repairs, ignition components and routine maintenance. Availability depends on the vehicle, repair scope, safe work location and parts. Call to discuss your job before scheduling.
