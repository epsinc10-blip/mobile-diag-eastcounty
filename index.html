Mobile Auto Repair at Your Location

East County is spread out. A tow from Ramona, Jamul or Alpine to a dealership can cost more than the diagnosis itself. We eliminate the tow and come to where your vehicle is sitting.

We work on everyday drivers, diesel work trucks, ranch rigs, and hybrid commuter cars. Whether the truck is parked at a job site off Wildcat Canyon Road, the family car won’t start in the Lakeside driveway, or you are looking at a used F-250 in Santee before you buy, we can scan, test and give you an honest assessment without you ever leaving your location.

Common issues we are called for in East County

  • Check engine light and OBD-II diagnostics for all makes and models
  • Intermittent misfires and rough idle that only act up when hot or under load
  • Pre-purchase inspections before buying a used truck or SUV from a private seller
  • Emissions and smog-failure diagnostics so you know the real cost before repairs
  • No-start and no-crank issues on gas and diesel engines
  • Diesel-specific problems including DPF, DEF, turbo, glow plug and injector faults
  • Hybrid battery health checks for older Prius, Camry and other common models
  • Trailer and vehicle lighting/wiring problems on ranch and tow vehicles
